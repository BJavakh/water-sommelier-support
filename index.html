Frequently asked questions

The camera won't open when I try to scan a bottle.

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll down to Water Sommelier, and make sure Camera access is enabled. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Water Sommelier → Permissions → Camera.

The app didn't recognize the bottle I scanned.

Our brand database is constantly growing. If a bottle isn't recognized, please email us the brand name and a photo of the label and we'll add it within a few days.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email admin@watersommelier.app from the address associated with your account and we'll remove your data within 7 days.

Where can I see what minerals are in my water?

After scanning a bottle, tap the result to view the full mineral breakdown, pH, and TDS information when available.

I have a feature request or feedback.

We'd love to hear from you. Send your thoughts to admin@watersommelier.app - real users shape what we build next.

Privacy

Water Sommelier uses your camera only to scan water bottle labels. Photos are processed for scan results and are not shared with third parties for advertising.
