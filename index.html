Editor's note
On the quiet matter of what we drink.

We drink the same water, day after day, with about as much attention as we give the air we breathe. Yet not all water is the same. Source matters. Mineral content matters. pH matters. A glass of alpine spring water carries a different chemistry — and a different story — than a glass from a city tap.

Water Sommelier began as a small idea: that anyone, with nothing more than a phone camera, ought to be able to know what is in their bottle. No chemistry degree. No flipping the label over and squinting at fine print.

This is a pocket guide for the curious drinker.

Chapter I.5 · the anatomy of water

What's inside the glass.

Water is never just water. It carries minerals from the rock it rose through, dissolved gases from the air, and a quiet electrical charge measured as pH. The app reads all of it.

  • H₂OTwo hydrogen, one oxygen. The molecule that does the dissolving.
  • Ca²⁺Calcium ions, leached from limestone. Builds bone density when you drink it.
  • Mg²⁺Magnesium. Quiet electrolyte, useful after a long run.
  • HCO₃⁻Bicarbonate. Buffers the water's pH and gives it a soft mouthfeel.
  • pHThe acid-alkaline balance. Above 7 is alkaline. Below 7 is acidic.

Chapter IV · correspondence with the sommelier

Ask the sommelier.

The app's AI assistant has read the literature, the labels, and the lab reports. Put a question to it.

SR
Best water for a long run?
WS
Answer 01 Look for high sodium and high calcium — Vichy Catalan, Gerolsteiner, or San Pellegrino all qualify. Avoid distilled or low-mineral waters; you'll lose electrolytes faster than you replace them.
SR
Is alkaline water actually good for me?
WS
Answer 02 The pH of what you drink barely changes your body's chemistry — your stomach is too acidic for it to matter. What does matter is the minerals. Most alkaline waters happen to be mineral-rich, which is the real benefit.
SR
What does TDS mean on a label?
WS
Answer 03 Total dissolved solids. The sum, in milligrams per litre, of everything in the water that isn't water. Below 50 is very low, 50–500 is normal, above 500 is mineral-rich. Higher isn't better — it's different.

Q.01 The camera won't open when I try to scan.
Open your device Settings, find Water Sommelier, and confirm that Camera access is enabled. On iOS: Settings → Water Sommelier → Camera. On Android: Settings → Apps → Water Sommelier → Permissions → Camera.
Q.02 My bottle wasn't recognized.
Our database grows every week. Send the brand name and a photograph of the label to admin@watersommelier.app, and we'll add it within a few days.
Q.03 How do I delete my account or data?
Write to admin@watersommelier.app from the address linked to your account. We'll permanently delete your data within seven days.
Q.04 Is Water Sommelier free?
Yes. Free to download, free to use. No advertising. We may, in time, offer a paid tier for advanced features — the basics will remain free forever.
Q.05 I have a feature request.
We'd love to hear it. Real user feedback drives every line of the roadmap. Write to admin@watersommelier.app — we read every word.
House rules

A small code of conduct.

Four principles we hold ourselves to.

i.

The camera is for labels.

Nothing is recorded or uploaded unless you tap to scan. The viewfinder isn't always listening.

ii.

No ads. Ever.

We don't sell your data, run ad networks, or share information with marketers. There is nothing to sell.

iii.

Your data is yours.

Scans, hydration logs, account email — stored securely, deleted on request within a week.

iv.

Anonymous, by default.

We measure crashes and broad usage to fix bugs and ship better. Nothing identifying, nothing creepy.
