Typical response: 1-2 business days
The camera won't open when I try to scan.
Open your device Settings, find Water Sommelier, and make sure Camera access is enabled. On iOS: Settings → Water Sommelier → Camera. On Android: Settings → Apps → Water Sommelier → Permissions → Camera.
My bottle wasn't recognized.
Our brand database grows every week. Email admin@watersommelier.app with the brand name and a photo of the label, and we'll add it within a few days.
How do I delete my account or data?
Email admin@watersommelier.app from the address linked to your account and we'll permanently delete your data within 7 days.
Is Water Sommelier free?
Yes. Water Sommelier is free to download and use, with no ads.
I have a feature request.
We'd love to hear it. Send your idea to admin@watersommelier.app. Real user feedback drives what we build next.
